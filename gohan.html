Regular timeline Gohan

Gohan is one of the main characters of the Dragon ball animated series because he is the son of the mainest character who is Goku. Gohan is pretty important in the story because he has lots of potential. His main power source is through emotion that Goku helps him unleash. For example when the villain named Cell killed a bunch of Gohan's friends, Gohan became way stronger and killed Cell. Another time is during the Super Dragon Ball Heroes movie, which was years later. The way stronger version of Cell really damaged Piccolo and got Gamma 1 killed.
