South Park

South Park is a popular animated series that has been releasing episodes for 29 years the show is about 4 young boys named Stan, Kyle, Cartman, and Kenny. The most appearing character is probably either Cartman or Stan, personally I think that it is Stan because he is the main protagonist and Cartman is the main Antagonist. I say this because Cartman is known for creating problems and Stan is usually there to help solve them with other main characters, like Kenny, Kyle, or other characters. This show is known for being offensive, funny and unique. This show is also known for being really controversial and has been banned in some countries. In my opinion, I like the show for its comedy even if it is offensive.

Stats

South Park the TV show was released on August 13th, 1997. The show South Park became popular right after its release. South Park was given a path of success right from the start. This show has 29 seasons and more will be arriving soon hopefully. Most of these seasons have different amounts of episodes, so the average amount of episodes per season is 10. The range is from 2 - 18 episodes but the 2 episode season is probably an outlier. The show has a total of 329 episodes that are each about 20 minutes. The show has really good ratings, because if you think about it the show has really controversial topics which would make us expect the rating to be really low. The season with the lowest rating average was season 28 of south park with a rating of 6.9/10 on IMBD. The season with the highest rating average in South Park was season 8 with a rating of 8.6/10 on IMBD. The episode of south park with the lowest rating is season 22 episode 6 with a rating of 6.4/10 on IMBD. This episode is called "The problem with the Poo". This episode is about cancel culture and more things. The episode of South Park with the highest rating is season 5 episode 4 with a rating of 9.6/10 on IMBD. This episode is called "Scott Tenorman Must Die". This episode is about Cartman getting revenge on his stepbrother Scott Tenorman who he feeds mystery chilli to. When we look at the ratings we can see that almost everyone who watches the show really loves it because the lowest rating is still decently high. So in conclusion we can say that this is a really good show, even if you don't like some of the episodes. You will probably like most of them.
