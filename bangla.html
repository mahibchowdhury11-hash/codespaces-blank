We will go over some of the history of Bangladesh on this page

Earning Independence

Before Bangladesh became a country, the people who wanted it to become one needed to fight for it. So a war happened, it started on March 16, 1971 and ended 9 months later on December 16, 1971. This war was named the Bangladesh Liberation War. In this war many people died, about 300 thousand to 3 million Bengalis were killed. About 200 thousand to 400 thousand were raped by the Pakistani military. This was a genocide against Bangladesh that happened during the Bangladesh Liberation War. All of these terrible things happened but in the end Bangladesh became an independant country.
